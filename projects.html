Medical AI & Machine Learning Research

Suggestive Strategies for Mitigating Adverse Effects in Drug-Drug Interactions (DDI)

Developed a deep learning framework (CNN vs DNN) predicting 65 DDI event types using DrugBank data, leveraging SMILES molecular structures, drug targets, and biological pathways with Jaccard similarity. The CNN model achieved 89.89% accuracy, 87.11% precision, and an 80.72% F1-score. Presented at ICRACE'24 and under review at IC&C 2026.

Amend Complementary & Integrative Health Approaches for Untangling Prevalent Health Issues

Conducted a comparative evaluation of six machine learning models (SVC, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, KNN, Naïve Bayes, Neural Networks) for symptom-based disease prediction across 41 diseases and 133 symptoms, achieving a top accuracy of 97.62%. Submitted to IC&C 2026.
